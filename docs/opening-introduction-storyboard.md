# At His Table: Your seat is here

Production-ready first draft. Approximately 60–75 seconds at a warm conversational pace. Use existing Heather and Emily likenesses. The live illustrated introduction is at /welcome; no video or voice has been generated yet.

| Time | Picture / screen | Dialogue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8 sec | Emily at the table; Heather listening. | Emily: “I want to spend time with Jesus. But some mornings, I don’t even know where to begin.” |
| 8–13 sec | Heather, warm and unhurried. | Heather: “Begin right where you are. There’s a seat for you.” |
| 13–26 sec | Actual three-question page. Gently highlight season, today’s need, available time. | Heather: “The first time, tell us a little about your season, what’s on your heart, and how much time you have. We’ll choose a devotional to help you get started.” |
| 26–30 sec | Emily glances toward the screen. | Emily: “And when I come back tomorrow?” |
| 30–46 sec | Today page: check-in, Scripture, prayer. Keep text readable; no rapid scrolling. | Heather: “Go straight to your time at the table. Tell us how you’re feeling that day, or simply begin. Praise Him, read His Word, and let the prayer help you put your heart into words.” |
| 46–50 sec | Reflection fields. | Emily: “Do I have to write something?” |
| 50–60 sec | Show Save and finish for today, then Finish for today — no writing needed. | Heather: “Only if you’d like. Keep a thought, offer a word of thanks, or finish here.” |
| 60–64 sec | Emily and Heather together. | Emily: “And if I miss a day?” |
| 64–69 sec | Heather smiles gently. | Heather: “Just come back. Your seat is still here.” |
| 69–75 sec | Brand end card; one Take Your Seat button. | On screen: “A little time. An open heart. A place to begin.” |

## Direction

Natural conversation; Heather guides and Emily asks short, relatable questions. Soft daylight and existing cream/plum identity. Use existing portraits for the still-image review before generating character footage. Use real interface captures for instructional scenes. Do not render UI text through a video model. Keep music below dialogue and include accurate captions and a transcript. Playback is optional, user-initiated, and replayable. Do not auto-start audio. Review the still sequence and timing before spending video-generation credits.
